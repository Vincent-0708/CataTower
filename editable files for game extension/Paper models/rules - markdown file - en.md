# **🎲 CataTower — Rules of the Game**  
Welcome to **CataTower**, the game where every block you place can be a triumph… or the beginning of the end\! Build the tower higher and higher, take bold risks, unleash devastating effects—but above all… don’t be the one who makes it fall.

## **🎯 Objective of the Game**  
Your mission: **stack pieces onto the tower without making it collapse**. Each block placed earns you points. The first player to **reach exactly 18 points** wins the game. But beware: if you go over 18, your score is **cut in half**\! In CataTower, you must be daring—without getting greedy.

## **🧩 Game Components**

* 31 building blocks:  
  * 🟩 **Green**: simple and stable  
  * 🟦 **Blue**: trickier to place  
  * 🟥 **Red**: for the brave  
  * ⚪ **White**: the Effect pieces  
  * ⚫ **The Unique Grey piece**: the explosive finale

* A deck of 17 **Effect cards** and 22 **Malus cards**  
* A score sheet to track player progress  
* A six-sided die  
* The game box (one half serves as the tower’s base, the other as the piece reserve)

## **🔧 Setup**

1. All players start at **0 points**.  
2. Place the blocks in one half of the box. The other half goes in the center as **the base of the tower**. The **first player** decides how to position the base and places the very first block on it.  
3. Play proceeds **clockwise**.

## **▶️ Taking Your Turn**  
 On your turn:

1. **Roll the dice**. If it lands on the “M” face, **draw a Malus card** and **apply it immediately**.  
2. Choose any remaining block.  
3. **Place it on the tower**, at least as high as the lowest block already placed.  
4. If the block is placed successfully:  
   * 🟩 Green: \+1 point  
   * 🟦 Blue: \+2 points  
   * 🟥 Red: \+5 points  
   * ⚪ Effect block: **draw an Effect card** (keep it secret). Play it at any time, or **before** placing your block if it applies.

**⚠️ Important Rules**

* Placing a block **on its flattest face** (see diagram at the end) scores 0 points. For Effect blocks, you also skip drawing a card.  
* All blocks placed directly on the base score 0 points.  
* Effect blocks cannot be the first block placed on the base.  
* **Only one tower can exist**. Every block must contribute to the same structure.  
* On the very first round, only Green blocks may be played (for 3 players or fewer, this applies to the first 2 rounds).

## **💥 When the Tower Shakes…**  
 If blocks fall:

* **Less than half the tower** falls (including the one you just placed):  
  * You lose **1 point per fallen block.**  
  * The round continues. Fallen blocks return to the reserve. Your next turn starts with the current tower state.

* **Half or more of the tower** falls (or the whole thing):  
  * You lose **1 point per fallen block.**  
  * **The round ends immediately**. All blocks go back to the reserve. **Malus cards are discarded**, but **Effect cards stay in play.**  
  * You reset the base however you like and place the first block of the new round (which never scores points).

⚠️ Scores can never go below 0\.

## **⚫ The Unique Grey Piece**  
 When all green, blue, red, and white blocks are gone, only **the Unique Grey piece remains**:

* If placed successfully, **all other players lose 10 points**. The player who placed it starts the next round.  
* If the tower collapses, the player loses points as usual (1 per fallen block).

## **🎴 Effect & Malus Cards**

* Effect and Malus cards create surprises, twists, and sabotage opportunities\!  
* A player may hold **a maximum of 2 Effect cards**.  
* If you draw a third, you must play one immediately.

## **🏆 End of the Game**  
 The game continues across rounds until a player reaches **exactly 18 points**. That player is crowned **Master of the Tower**\!  
 ⚠️ If a player overshoots 18, their score is halved and the game goes on.

## **➕ Bonus Rule (Expert Mode)**  
 In “+ Mode,” players agree on a time limit (e.g., 5 seconds) to place their block. If they fail to meet the limit, they draw a Malus.

👉 **Now it’s your turn… may the Tower be ever in your favor\!**
